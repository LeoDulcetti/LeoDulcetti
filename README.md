## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an **AI Engineer at IBM** and an **Associate Professor at Universidad Pontificia Comillas (ICAI-ICADE) and Universidad Alfonso X el Sabio (UAX)**, based in Madrid.

My work focuses on **Generative AI, Agentic AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**. I build AI-powered applications and multi-agent workflows, combining hands-on engineering with a background in **data science, machine learning, and finance**.

Alongside my industry work, I teach **Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and Time Series Analysis**. I enjoy turning cutting-edge AI concepts into practical tools and sharing that knowledge with the next generation of AI professionals.
